Frequently Asked Questions

How does two moves sync my daily activity?

two moves uses native device Health integrations (Apple HealthKit / Google Fit) to log your movement effortlessly. All data is processed locally to protect your privacy.

Is my data backed up securely?

Yes. Your daily habit progress and principles reflections can be synced to your private iCloud or Google Account. We do not store your private notes on third-party servers.

How do I restore my purchase or subscription?

Open the App Settings within two moves or My Principles, scroll to the Account section, and tap "Restore Purchases" to reactivate your access on any device linked to your Apple ID or Google account.

How can I request a new feature or report a bug?

We love user feedback! Please send your feature ideas or bug descriptions directly to our support team email below. We reply to all inquiries within 24 hours.
