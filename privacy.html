Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At wellnesskits ("we", "our", or "us"), respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data is fundamental to how we build our applications, including two moves, My Principles, and future products. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize a privacy-first architecture. Most of your data stays locally on your device:

  • Local App Data: Activity logs in two moves and reflections in My Principles are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.
  • Usage & Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic and performance data (e.g., crash logs) to improve app stability and user experience.
  • Contact Information: If you voluntarily reach out to our support team, we receive your email address and message contents solely to assist you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data exclusively for:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our applications.
  • Responding to customer support inquiries and user feedback.
  • Ensuring app stability, fixing bugs, and preventing security breaches.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers. We may only share anonymized data with reliable service providers (such as Apple App Store, Google Play Services, or crash reporting tools) solely for app infrastructure operation.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. For apps with cloud backup, your data is transmitted securely via SSL/TLS encryption.

5. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete your local app data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • You may request data export or deletion by contacting our privacy team.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@wellnesskits.app
