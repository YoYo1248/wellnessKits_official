Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At WellnessKits ("we", "our", or "us"), respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data is fundamental to how we build our applications, including Two Moves!, My Principles, and future tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Science into Daily Practice, Privacy First

We prioritize a privacy-first architecture. Most of your data stays locally on your device:

  • Local App Data: Movement logs in Two Moves! and decision principles in My Principles are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic data (e.g., crash logs) to maintain app stability and performance.
  • Support Inquiries: If you voluntarily email support, we receive your email address solely to assist you.

2. How We Use Information

We use non-personal diagnostic data strictly for maintaining quality, fixing bugs, and improving user experience.

3. Data Protection

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries, please visit our Support Center.
