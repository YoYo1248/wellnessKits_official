Privacy Policy for Two Moves

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WellnessKits (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Two Moves mobile application and its related services (the “Service”).

1. Summary

  • Most of your timers, settings, reminders, and activity history stay on your device.
  • The optional AI feature sends the text you choose to submit to our server and to an external AI provider.
  • We process limited technical identifiers and purchase information to protect the Service, enforce usage limits, and verify subscriptions.
  • We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.
  • Two Moves is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

2. Information stored on your device

Two Moves stores app data locally on your device, including saved timers and projects, app settings and language, reminder preferences, onboarding status, Move progress and history, widget information, AI consent status, AI drafts, AI-generated timers, and local usage information. This data is used to provide the app’s features and is not routinely uploaded to us.

Local notifications are scheduled through Apple’s notification system after you choose to enable them. Two Moves does not require notification permission in order to use the app.

3. Optional AI feature

The AI feature is optional. Before using it, the app asks for permission to send the text you enter to an external AI provider. When you submit text, we send the text, along with limited technical request information, through our backend to:

  • DeepSeek for users routed through our mainland China service; or
  • Google Gemini for users routed through our global service.

The provider processes the text to generate a structured timer or exercise-snack plan. Our backend does not intentionally save the raw text after completing the request. A copy of your draft and generated result may remain on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

AI providers may process prompts, outputs, IP addresses, usage details, and safety or diagnostic information under their own terms. Google’s handling depends on the applicable Gemini service tier and region. For unpaid Gemini API services outside the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, Google states that submitted content and generated responses may be used to improve its products and may be reviewed by humans. For paid Gemini API services, Google states that it does not use prompts and responses to improve its products, but may log them for a limited period for safety, security, and legal purposes. DeepSeek processes end-user input to generate output and may retain relevant records where required for safety, compliance, or legal obligations.

Do not submit names, identification numbers, financial details, precise addresses, medical records, or other sensitive or confidential information. Do not submit another person’s personal information unless you have their permission. AI output may contain errors and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

4. Technical, security, and usage information

When you use the AI feature or contact our backend, we may process:

  • a randomly generated device identifier;
  • your IP address and approximate service region;
  • request time, selected provider, model, token or character counts, response status, latency, and error information; and
  • usage counters used to enforce per-minute, daily, and monthly limits and emergency cost controls.

We use keyed hashes of device identifiers and IP addresses in our rate-limit storage rather than storing the raw values in rate-limit keys. We use this information to provide the Service, prevent abuse, secure our systems, enforce plan limits, diagnose failures, and control unexpected AI costs. We do not use it for cross-app advertising or user profiling.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. To verify and maintain access to paid features, we receive and store limited App Store transaction and subscription information, which may include product ID, transaction ID, original transaction ID, expiration date, renewal status, refund or revocation status, storefront environment, and Apple server-notification identifiers. We associate this information with a protected device identifier so the app can restore and check entitlement status.

6. Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request, investigate problems, protect the Service, and meet legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive health or identity information by email.

7. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide timers, AI generation, subscriptions, reminders, and other requested features;
  • verify purchases and restore paid access;
  • enforce free and paid usage allowances;
  • detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, and unexpected cost spikes;
  • debug, maintain, and improve the reliability of the Service;
  • respond to support and privacy requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our terms.

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on performance of our contract to provide requested app and subscription features, your consent for optional AI text sharing, our legitimate interests in securing and operating the Service, and legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw AI consent in the app, but withdrawal does not affect processing already completed.

8. Service providers and disclosures

We disclose information only as needed to operate the Service, honor your choices, or comply with law. Our service providers currently include:

  • Apple, for App Store distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and server notifications;
  • Google Gemini, for global AI processing;
  • DeepSeek, for mainland China AI processing;
  • Vercel, for global hosting and serverless functions;
  • Tencent Cloud, for mainland China hosting and serverless functions;
  • Upstash, for protected usage-limit and entitlement records; and
  • Google/Gmail and email-delivery providers, for support and operational communications.

These providers process information on our behalf or as otherwise described in their terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

9. Retention

  • Local app data: remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, subject to device backups controlled by you or Apple.
  • Raw AI text: is not intentionally retained by our backend after the request finishes. AI providers may retain or process it according to their applicable terms, service tier, and legal obligations.
  • Usage protection records: minute and daily counters generally expire after their relevant limit window. Monthly counters generally expire after the following monthly reset, with a short operational buffer.
  • Purchase records: expired subscription records may be retained for up to two years after expiration. Lifetime-purchase and device-to-purchase records may be retained for up to ten years to support restoration, refunds, fraud prevention, and legal or accounting requirements. Apple notification deduplication identifiers may be kept for up to 200 days.
  • Support messages: are kept for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, protect the Service, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not currently promise a fixed deletion period for support correspondence.

We may retain information longer when required by law, necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements, or needed to protect the security and integrity of the Service. Where feasible, we delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

10. International data transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than where you live. In particular, mainland China AI requests are processed through DeepSeek and Tencent Cloud in the People’s Republic of China, while global requests may be processed by providers in other countries. Where required by applicable law, we use or rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

11. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the core timer features without enabling AI.
  • You can withdraw AI consent in the app and stop submitting new AI text.
  • You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete locally stored timers and projects in the app or remove local app data by uninstalling the app.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, to withdraw consent, or to appeal a decision. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email support.wellnesskits@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. Some records cannot be deleted immediately when they are required for purchase restoration, fraud prevention, security, accounting, or legal compliance.

12. No sale, advertising, or cross-app tracking

We do not sell or rent personal information. Two Moves does not include third-party advertising, and we do not use personal information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for targeted advertising.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, restricted server credentials, protected identifiers, rate limiting, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Adults only

Two Moves is intended for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information through the Service, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Service, our providers, or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy and update the date at the top. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
